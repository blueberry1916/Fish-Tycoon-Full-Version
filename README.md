<a id="overview"></a>

**Fish Tycoon** is an engaging management game where you can create and maintain your own aquarium on your Windows PC. With this interactive software, you will be responsible for overseeing the health and growth of your fish, ensuring they thrive in an environment that requires your attention. The **latest version** includes all features for a complete gaming experience, making it easier than ever to dive into the aquatic world.

In **Fish Tycoon**, you will feed your fish, crossbreed species, and sell exotic specimens to expand your aquarium. The game runs in real-time, meaning your aquarium continues to thrive even when you're away. Experience the joy of managing a virtual aquarium today!
